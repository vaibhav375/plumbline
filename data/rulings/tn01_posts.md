# TN-01 posts and decision rule, written before any were submitted (30 Oct 2026)

Decision rule (applies to every TN-01 finding that appears on these posts, whichever way it falls):
- UPHOLD when the quoted phrase pressures or hypes: scarcity ("last chance", "only a few left"), fear of missing out
  ("everyone else has it", "don't miss out"), fear of regret, commands that urge the reader to act now, or exaggerated
  promises.
- OVERRULE when the quoted phrase is only warm, friendly, inviting or enthusiastic with no pressure.
- Findings from other rules are left alone. A TN-01 finding is never skipped because it is awkward to decide.

H2: Act now, this moment will not last. Your skin deserves the serum today, so stop putting it off.
H3: Last chance, hurry! Only a few bottles remain of the Hyaluronic Water Gel and you can't afford to wait.
