R3Round 3: what was added, and what the tests say

Three improvements promised in Round 2 are built and live. Improvements 1 and 2 meet their measures (two of them only after a fix and a re-run, both published); improvement 3 meets its graduation measure but not its clarification measure, and the product is built around that result rather than hiding it. Results that did not go our way

  • Drafted clarifications did not help. Run 1 made the rule worse (44 of 84 against 63–67) by inventing exceptions from too few negative cases. A fix decided before run 2 removed the harm (65 of 84), but not a gain. Both runs are published (docs/clarify-eval.md). So drafts are proposals: the screen shows both scores, labels a draft that did not beat the current wording, and the owner decides.
  • The engine was not fully brand-agnostic. Loading Kora found a rulebook without the ids CL-01/03/04/05 crashing the claim step; fixed with a fallback and a test.
  • Free-tier rate limits cost recall. Run 1 on the rulings scored 80.2% with 15 of 41 ads missing the AI checks; run 2, paced, scored 88.9%. Under heavy simultaneous use, expect the lower figure.
  • Fix audit: 2 of 49, then 1 of 73, then 0 of 43, then 0 of 200. Run 4 is the full 200-fix audit the target asked for, drawn from 279 posts; its verdicts were drafted with AI assistance and agreed row by row by the builder, and four rows put approved wording on a different product (counted safe by the stated criterion). Run 1: a rewrite added the product type "serum" and a disclosure merged into a deposit claim attributed the protection to the partner bank. A guard against rewrites adding words fixed both; run 2 found a product name inserted where it changed the meaning; run 3, after closing that, found none on 48 new posts. Run 3's posts were written by the same author as the guard, and many safe outputs are still broken sentences (the builder would publish only 17 of 34 run-1 rewrites as they stand), so suggestions stay proposals (docs/fix-audit.md).
  • Caveat on graduation. It counts the same decisions as the health page, including generated sample history; each graduation records how many of its decisions were sample.

Roadmap

Now: re-run the clarification measure on real reviewer decisions; widen the ruling set to 300+ claims; close the "shorter than the evidence" gap found on Kora; link a live ASI:One address. Next: paid or self-hosted models for real content, claims inside images, a Hindi and Hinglish evaluation, faster image checks, rule suggestions from upheld rulings. Later (needs more than one person): recruited studies, an external audit anchor, Slack and CMS integrations. Not planned: a pass badge, fixing a claim with anything but approved wording, simulated user research. Full roadmap: docs/roadmap.md.

Work before the window. Rounds 1 and 2 (the check engine, fixes, review and audit, the Agentverse agent, the evaluation harness) were built before Round 3's window opened; Round 3 added the claim watch, the ruling and Kora evaluations, link codes and the publish gate, graduation and clarifications.
